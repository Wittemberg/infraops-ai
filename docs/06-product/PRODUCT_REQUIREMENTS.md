- nodes;
- agent heartbeat/inventory;
- CPU/RAM/disk/uptime;
- saúde e expectativa de backup;
- alerts;
- jobs/actions seguras;
- Proxmox;
- Virtualizor;
- IA read-only e posteriormente IA + Actions;
- portal de cliente read-only.
