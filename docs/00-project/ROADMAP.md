# Roadmap — InfraOps AI

> **Estado atual:** Etapas 1–23 concluídas. **Próximas:** Etapa 24 Goal-Oriented Operations e Etapa 25 Infrastructure Intelligence & Continuous Improvement.

## Etapas 1–20 — Fundação
Core, Agents, multi-tenancy, RBAC, observabilidade, Proxmox, Virtualizor, Backup Engine, IA, Actions, Policy Engine, auditoria e deploy.

## Etapa 21 — Autonomous Scheduler 🟢
Cron/interval/one-shot, timezone, presets, Run Now e histórico.

## Etapa 22 — Conditional Triggers 🟢
Telemetria/eventos, debounce, cooldown, circuit breaker, dedup e simulação.

## Etapa 23 — Autonomous Policies & Self-Healing 🟢
Autonomy matrix, risk budget, evidence threshold, pre/postcheck, escalation e self-healing history.

## Etapa 24 — Goal-Oriented Infrastructure Management 🟡
Goals/SLOs declarativos, drift, budgets, Actions permitidas e validação contínua.

## Etapa 25 — Infrastructure Intelligence & Continuous Improvement ⚪
1. Recurring Incident Analysis
2. Root Cause Pattern Mining
3. Infrastructure Recommendations
4. Capacity Forecasting
5. Single Point of Failure Detection
6. Technical Debt Score
7. Cost / ROI Analysis
8. Lifecycle & Upgrade Advisor
9. Recommendation Validation Loop
10. Executive Infrastructure Review

### Gate
Recomendações isoladas por tenant, evidence-backed, confidence calculado, sem mudança estrutural direta, custos não inventados, lifecycle auditável e before/after validado.

> **Self-Healing resolve o incidente. Infrastructure Intelligence tenta impedir que ele volte a existir.**
